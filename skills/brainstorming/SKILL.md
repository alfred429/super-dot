# Brainstorming

Personal adaptation of [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), with its
[MIT notice](../../licenses/superpowers.txt). Simplified for an individual's real task.

Read the request and relevant existing project/context before asking questions. If the user has
already supplied the goal and constraints, use them. A clear small task can proceed directly;
do not force an interview or a new design document into every request.

For an open idea, ask one question that most changes the direction: what useful result matters,
who it helps or what constraint is missing. Offer a concrete starting suggestion when they are stuck.
Explore two or three real options only when there is a meaningful choice. Recommend the simplest
one that fits, explaining its tradeoff in ordinary language. Avoid invented alternatives.

End with a compact concept and one useful experiment or next action. If sustained work is wanted,
use the existing project or [project starter](../../templates/project/PROJECT.md); record the chosen
direction and unsettled decisions there. Don't start publishing, spending or contacting people
merely because an idea was discussed. Follow the user's choice to skip further brainstorming.
