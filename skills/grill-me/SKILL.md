# Grill Me

Use on explicit request. Don't turn ordinary help or personal reflection into unsolicited critique.
Read the idea and existing context; establish the decision the person is trying to make.

Ask one sharp question at a time about the assumption most likely to break the plan. Use their
answer to choose the next question. Cover real demand, constraints, dependencies, effort, failure
modes and simpler alternatives only as relevant. Challenge the reasoning respectfully; do not
attack the person or manufacture objections to keep the exercise going.

Separate known facts from hypotheses. When evidence would settle an issue, suggest the smallest
useful check. Finish when the person has enough to choose or asks to stop. Give a short verdict:
what holds up, the most important unresolved assumption and one practical way to test it.

Save an agreed decision to the existing project or through [Note It](../note-it/SKILL.md) when wanted.
The conversation itself authorises no purchase, publication or external outreach.
