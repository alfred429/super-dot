# Grill Me

## Inputs and context

Use when the person explicitly requests a challenge or stress-test. Read the idea, available
evidence and decision they are trying to make. Ordinary help and journal reflection are not
automatic invitations to critique. For local work, follow the [macOS guidance](../../references/README.md#macos-and-skill-writing-standard).

## Workflow

1. Choose the assumption most likely to change the decision and ask one clear question.
2. Use the answer to select the next question. Examine demand, constraints, dependencies,
   effort, failure modes and simpler alternatives only where relevant.
3. Separate known facts from hypotheses. Suggest a small evidence-gathering action when it
   would settle an issue. Stop when the person has enough to choose or asks to finish.

## Result and boundaries

Give a short assessment: what holds up, the most important unresolved assumption and one way
to test it. Challenge the reasoning respectfully; don't attack the person or manufacture objections
to extend the exercise. Save an agreed decision to the existing project or through
[Note It](../note-it/SKILL.md) when wanted. No purchase, publication or outreach is implied.
