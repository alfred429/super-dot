# My SuperDot — start here

Template version: 1.0.0 · 2026-10-31

Your knowledge, projects and journal share one home. Capture something useful, put it in the right
life area, and link it to related projects or reflections. Start with a sentence; organise as needed.

Open `index.md` for navigation. `AGENTS.md` contains the ten agentic principles. `SCHEMA.md`
explains the few note/source conventions. The assistant reads these before working in this vault.

## Make it yours

- EDIT: Preferred name — not set.
- EDIT: Timezone — not set; confirm before scheduling.
- EDIT: Current priorities — choose up to three, or leave for later.
- EDIT: Communication — concise, kind, concrete; one next action when overwhelmed.
- EDIT: Privacy boundaries — private by default; no external sharing without clear permission.
- EDIT: Journal inputs — selected available conversation material; other sources require opt-in.
- EDIT: Routines — morning/evening and vault care on request until times are chosen.
- EDIT: Connections — record chosen accounts/services and their status, never secret values.

Example: “Call me Alex. Europe/London. Help me finish my portfolio; keep family notes private.”
That is enough to start. Change any default when it stops helping.

## Life areas

Use `Life/Personal`, `Life/Family`, `Life/Work`, `Life/Hobbies`, `Life/Money` and `Life/Wellbeing`.
Each has a short README and assistant context. Add notes directly or make Knowledge/Projects
subfolders when useful. Copy `System/Templates/Project` into the relevant area's Projects folder
for an active outcome; update its name, goal and next action. An interest can remain a note.

## The connected loop

Conversation or chosen source → Note It → life-area knowledge ↔ projects/actions → journal →
morning priorities → new conversations and outcomes. One main home per item; links join areas.
Notion and creative tools receive selected outputs, rather than a mirror of your private life.
