# Notion — selected notes, content and sharing

Version: 1.1.0 · 2026-10-31

Keep the private vault as the knowledge home. Use Notion for content the person deliberately
wants to organise, collaborate on or share; a connected account is not permission to mirror it all.

1. Reuse an existing Notion account/workspace. With permission, install/enable the supported Notion
   connection in the user's app and complete its private authorisation flow.
2. Choose the actual destination page and audience. Read that destination before writing and
   confirm the intended account/workspace. Keep a first draft private unless sharing is authorised.
3. Draft one useful selected item: a note, project summary or content outline. Inspect the saved
   page and return its link. A successful login alone doesn't demonstrate content creation.
4. For later updates, read the page first and preserve personal edits. Keep the vault's source link
   and identify which object owns the published copy; avoid two competing working masters.

[Notion's official MCP guide](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/get-started-with-mcp)
documents supported client setup and OAuth. If a native connector is unavailable, consult that
guide for the actual host's supported route instead of treating Codex-specific config as a Dot API.
Authorisation may expose broader workspace content than the intended destination; inspect the
granted scope and keep usage within the person's choice.

Example: turn a chosen project note into a private article draft, then ask where to share it.
Emailing, publishing and changing access require the user's clear authorisation.

For ongoing organisation, use [Notion Organizer](../skills/notion-organizer/SKILL.md) on the chosen
pages/databases. It preserves content and reports proposed structural changes for approval; schedule
it only through the person's chosen [routine settings](../SCHEDULE.md).
